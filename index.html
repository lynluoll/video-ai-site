AdTech: agents everywhere, assets nowhere. AdTech：到处是 Agent，素材却跟不上。

The buying interface used to ask for creative. Now it generates it. Once generation sits inside the buying loop, every campaign launch is a model call — and the volume tracks ad spend, not headcount. 投放界面过去是"向你要素材"，现在是"自己生成素材"。一旦生成进了投放主循环，每次起量都是一次模型调用——调用量跟着广告花费走，而不是跟着人头走。

Independent platforms follow, by buying in独立平台跟进的方式是"买进来"

One route we have seen repeatedly: acquire a generative creative startup outright rather than build the capability, then wire it into the existing buying stack. 我们反复看到的一条路径：与其自建，不如直接收购一家生成式创意公司，再接进已有的投放链路。

Most aggregate models rather than train them多数选择聚合模型，而不是自己训

The tooling layer is settling on multi-model routing — quality and cost per asset decide which engine gets the call, and that decision is revisited constantly. 工具层正在收敛到多模型路由——按质量与单条成本决定这次调用给谁，而且这个决定会被反复重估。

Which makes the engine slot contestable这让"引擎位"始终可争夺

Nobody in this layer is locked in. The engine behind the button is chosen on cost per asset, throughput and how fast a model can be swapped — and re-chosen whenever one of the three moves. 这一层没有人被锁死。按钮背后的引擎按单条成本、吞吐和换模型的速度来选——三者中任何一个变了，就会重新选一次。
